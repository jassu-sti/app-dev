# My Favorite Movies

1. **Captain America: Civil War**

   A Marvel superhero movie about the conflict between Captain America and Iron Man over the government's control of superheroes.

2. **Avengers: Infinity War**

   A superhero movie where the Avengers and their allies attempt to stop Thanos from collecting all six Infinity Stones.

3. **Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse**

   An animated superhero movie about Miles Morales, who discovers his powers and meets different versions of Spider-Man from alternate universes.

4. **The Wind Rises**

   An animated film by Studio Ghibli about Jiro Horikoshi, an aspiring aircraft designer who pursues his dream while facing the challenges of life, love, and war.

5. **White Chicks**

   A comedy movie about two FBI agents who go undercover by disguising themselves as wealthy socialites to solve a kidnapping case.
